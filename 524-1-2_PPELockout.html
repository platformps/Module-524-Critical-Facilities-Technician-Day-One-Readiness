GLAB 524.1.2

PPE Selection and Multi-Source Lockout

You read an arc flash label and a work order, select PPE against the label, then isolate a load on the Mobile Infrastructure Rack that is fed from more than one source. Proving the isolation dead with a meter is the next lab, GLAB 524.1.3 — a lock makes a circuit likely to be dead, not dead. The hazard in a multi-source isolation is the source you did not count, so the written record of what you isolated matters as much as the locks you hung.

How this lab is submitted

  • Your handout, GLAB 524.1.2 - PPE Selection and Multi-Source Lockout, carries the scenario, the equipment list and the step-by-step instructions. Work from it.
  • Press Packet in the bar above for the work order packet: the work order, the arc flash label on the assigned panel, the one-line diagram, and the lockout log and PPE selection you fill in as you go. What you type there is captured into your submission PDF. Print packet gives you a paper copy to carry to the rack.
  • Every question in that handout is reproduced below. Answer them here, not on the paper.
  • Steps carried out on the floor — the partner check, donning PPE in sequence, operating the disconnects, hanging locks and tags, releasing stored energy — are observed and signed off by your instructor against the handout.
  • When you are finished, press Build submission PDF, save it, and upload the single file to the 524.1.2 assignment in Canvas.
