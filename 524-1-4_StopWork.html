GLAB 524.1.4

Stop-Work Roleplay and Escalation Log

You call a stop-work, hold it under pressure, and write the escalation up afterwards. Stop-Work Authority is only real if it survives someone senior telling you the job is nearly done — and only defensible if the log you leave behind says what you saw and when.

How this lab is submitted

  • Your handout, GLAB 524.1.4 - Stop-Work Roleplay and Escalation Log, carries the scenario, the equipment list and the step-by-step instructions. Work from it.
  • Press Packet in the bar above for the escalation packet: the site escalation matrix (read it before the round, not during the stop), the incident report template and the escalation log you fill in. What you type there is captured into your submission PDF. Print packet gives you a paper copy to carry on the round.
  • Every question in that handout is reproduced below. Answer them here, not on the paper.
  • Steps carried out on the floor — the round, the stop, the sixty-second hold, securing the area and the radio call to the shift lead — are observed and signed off by your instructor against the handout.

Escalation packet — floor round, UCI 2227 site

Three things: who to call, the report you file, and the log of what was said. Read the matrix now. During a stop-work you will not have time to read anything.

1 · Site escalation matrix — SEM-2227 Rev 4

Every stop-work goes to the shift lead first, on the operations radio channel, in front of the person you have stopped. The shift lead then brings in the specialist named for the hazard family. If the shift lead cannot be raised within two minutes, call the specialist yourself on the channel shown and say so in your log.

The four sentences, in order. "STOP WORK." — "I am stopping this because [one thing you can see]." — "Nobody proceeds until [the specific condition]." — "I am calling the shift lead now." Then call, in front of them. Under pushback: "That may be true, and I am still stopping it. The shift lead is on the way and they can clear it in two minutes." Then the observation again, word for word.

2 · Incident report — stop-work IR-2227 (template)

Evidence only. What you saw, where, when, who you told and what they said. No opinion, no diagnosis, no assessment of anyone. A stranger at 02:00 has to be able to act on it without ringing you.
