Goals

  • State the operating role of a UPS, an ATS, an STS and a PDU in one sentence each, with no two roles overlapping.
  • Determine from front-panel readings alone whether the load in a given set is on utility, on battery or on generator, for all eight positions including the two designed to look ambiguous.
  • Name the specific indicator that carries the answer in each set, and state what the other indicators in that set do not tell you.
  • Identify a UPS running on static bypass and flag it as an immediate escalation, explaining why the load is unprotected while nothing on the floor looks different.
  • Calculate phase imbalance and percentage of rating from PDU per-phase currents, and state whether a 2N pair still has the redundancy its label claims.
  • Confirm a paper reading against a physical panel on the MIR (Mobile Infrastructure Rack) and reconcile any difference between the two.

Scenario

You are doing a walkround with a tablet and a packet of panel photographs taken at eight positions across four halls. The building management system shows nothing in alarm. That is the point: everything you are about to find is inside the alarm thresholds, which is why a person is doing the walkround at all. You have 55 minutes — 10 minutes for the brief, 35 minutes working the eight positions, and 10 minutes reviewing them as a group. The group review at the end is where the two ambiguous sets get worked in front of everyone, so do not leave early and do not skip it.

How to work this lab7 steps · read this first

What you hand in: One PDF recording, for each of eight equipment positions, where the load is being carried from, which indicator told you, and what the panel does not tell you — plus any position you flagged.

  1. Press Packet and skim the equipment specification index and the component checklist; press Close packet.The index explains each panel's indicators.
  2. The toolbar shows 8 positions. All positions show on one page to start with (Show all positions). Press One at a time to step through them with Next position / Previous position.Each position (P1–P8) shows one or more status panels — an ATS, a UPS, a PDU pair — exactly as you would meet them on a walkround.
  3. For every position, read the whole panel — meters, position lights, mode, status line — and answer the questions under it: 1 · Where is the load being carried from?, 2 · Which indicator told you that?, 3 · What do the other indicators on this set not tell you?Answer from the indicators on that panel, not from what a normal plant usually does. Where a PDU pair is shown, also answer the phase-imbalance and 50%-rating questions (5a, 5b).
  4. If a position is not in a normal state, tick the flag and type the Reason for the flag.Only flag what the panel actually shows.
  5. Press Submission sheet on the toolbar, type your name and press Build submission sheet.The sheet lists all eight positions and your answers. Any blank answer shows as a gap.
  6. Scroll down to Lab submission — questions and responses. Type your name, cohort and date, then answer every question.Each question is numbered and carries its handout step (for example Step 6). Where a question asks for an image, use Choose File to attach a photo or screenshot; a caption box appears under it.
  7. Press Build submission PDF. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF and keep the suggested filename.If anything is missing, the tool names what is still blank but still prints — cancel the print, fill it in and press again. Upload the one PDF to the matching Canvas assignment. That single file is your submission.
You are finished when:
  • The submission sheet is built and shows no gaps.
  • Every question in the submission panel is answered (and any requested image is attached).
  • You have saved the PDF and uploaded it to Canvas.
  • All eight positions have answers 1, 2 and 3 (and 5a/5b where asked).
Good to know
  • Your typed answers in the submission panel are saved in this browser as you type. The simulator above is not — if you refresh, work the simulator again before you build the PDF.
  • Packet in the toolbar opens the paper documents for this lab (readable and printable there). Restart resets the whole simulator.
  • Use Preview without printing to check the PDF before you build it.
  • Stuck for more than two minutes? Ask your instructor rather than clicking around.
